01 • FIND

Find Your People

Identify people and communities where a genuine conversation could exist.

LEARNGood prospecting is not about contacting everyone. Look for genuine relevance: existing relationships, shared interests, communities, life stages and reasons to reconnect.
PRACTISEChoose 5 people from your Launch List. For each, note the genuine reason you could start a conversation.
02 • CONNECT

Have Genuine Conversations

Build rapport without turning every interaction into a pitch.

LEARNConnection is a skill. Respond to what is actually happening in someone's world. Ask questions. Listen. Be interested without secretly steering every conversation toward an outcome.
PRACTISEStart 3 genuine conversations this week. No pitch required. Your job is to practise being present and curious.
03 • DISCOVER

Understand What Matters

Ask useful questions before deciding whether the opportunity is relevant.

LEARNDiscovery is about understanding, not interrogating. Explore what someone wants to change, create, protect or experience. Listen for goals, priorities, timing and willingness to learn.
PRACTISEUse one open question in a real conversation, then listen instead of preparing your pitch.
04 • INVITE

Offer the Right Next Step

Invite rather than pressure. Give people enough context to choose what happens next.

LEARNWhen there is genuine relevance, ask permission before sharing information. Match the resource to the person\'s stage: conversation, short video, fuller presentation or 3-way support. If there is no fit, respect that.
PRACTISEWrite one natural invitation in your own words. Keep it short and pressure-free.
05 • BUILD

Follow Up & Repeat

Turn isolated activity into a simple rhythm of learning, action and improvement.

LEARNProfessional follow-up is about keeping commitments, checking in appropriately and helping someone take the next useful step. It is not repeated pressure.
PRACTISEChoose three weekly actions that are measurable and realistic alongside your existing life.

📱 PERSONAL VIDEO

Make it personal.

A short video you record yourself to answer a specific question or continue a genuine conversation.

PERSONAL VIDEO FORMULA

1. Mention what they actually asked or shared.
2. Give one clear answer.
3. Keep it short and human.
4. End with one simple next step.

Don't record a generic pitch and send it to everyone. Make the video relevant to the conversation.

🧭 WHICH RESOURCE?

Quick Decision Guide

JUST CURIOUSKeep the conversation going. If they want a quick overview, use the Short Opportunity Video.
GENUINELY INTERESTEDThey've asked questions, understand the basic idea and want fuller context → use the ~23-minute presentation.
HAS SPECIFIC QUESTIONSAnswer the question yourself if you can, or use a Personal Video to explain it clearly.
NEEDS EXTRA SUPPORTUse a 3-Way Conversation when another builder/leader can genuinely add useful context.
NOT INTERESTED / NOT A FITRespect the answer. Keep the relationship intact. There is no need to force a next step.
💬 CONVERSATION TOOLS

DDISCOVER

EXAMPLE

Observe: “That place looks amazing.”
Connect: “I've been meaning to try somewhere like that.”
Explore: “What was it like?”
Discover: “Do you travel much outside of work?”
Invite: Only if the conversation reveals genuine relevance: “Would you be open to seeing what I'm building?”

The words will change. The principle stays the same: genuine conversation before opportunity.

🤝 3-WAY SUPPORT

Don't try to answer everything yourself.

BEFORETell your support person who you're speaking with, what they've shared, what they've asked and what outcome the person agreed to.
DURINGLet the person speak. Your support person adds context. Avoid creating a pile-on or overwhelming the prospect.
AFTERThank the person, ask what they thought and agree the appropriate next step — if there is one.
📱 SOCIAL MEDIA TOOLS

Build your personal brand.

CONTENTShare your journey, travel, lessons, lifestyle, mindset and useful information. Don't make every post an advert.
CONVERSATIONSUse Stories and posts as opportunities for genuine connection, not automated engagement bait.
VIDEOUse short-form video to become more comfortable communicating your ideas in your own voice.
TOOLSCanva • CapCut • your phone camera • Goodvibes HQ templates and prompts.
🔄 FOLLOW-UP

Professional persistence.

AGREED FOLLOW-UPIf someone says “I'll watch it tonight,” agree when you'll reconnect. Then keep the commitment.
QUESTION FOLLOW-UPIf they've asked a question, answer it or tell them when you'll get the answer. Don't disappear.
NO RESPONSEGive people space. Follow up appropriately rather than sending repeated messages until they respond.
NOA “no” is information. Respect it and move on without making the relationship uncomfortable.
📋 COPY-READY PROMPTS

Use these as starting points — not scripts.

SHORT VIDEO

“Hey, based on what we were talking about, I thought this might give you a quick idea of what I've been building. Have a look when you get a chance and let me know what you think.”

FULL PRESENTATION

“You've asked me a few questions about what I'm doing. I've got a longer presentation that explains the model properly. If you're genuinely open to looking at it, I'll send it over and we can talk afterwards.”

FOLLOW-UP

“Hey, just checking in as promised. Did you get a chance to look at it? No pressure either way — I'm happy to answer any questions you have.”

🧠 BEFORE YOU SEND ANYTHING

Ask yourself three questions.

1Have I actually had a conversation with this person?
2Do I understand why this resource is relevant to them?
3Have I given them enough context to make their own choice?

02 • COACH, DON'T CARRY

The coaching ladder

Use the support ladder before jumping straight into doing the work for someone.

LEVEL 1 • SELF-SERVEAcademy, tools and approved information.
LEVEL 2 • TEAM SUPPORTPeer discussion, examples and shared learning.
LEVEL 3 • LEADER COACHINGBring a specific situation, question or decision that needs leadership input.
THE AIMLeave the person more capable than when the conversation started.
03 • 1-TO-1 COACHING

Run a better coaching conversation.

ASK → LISTEN → CLARIFY → COACH → AGREE → FOLLOW UPDon't rush to solve. Understand first, then help the person identify their own next action.
04 • TRAFFIC-LIGHT TEAM CHECK-IN

Know where your people are.

This is a coaching tool, not a ranking system. Use it to decide what kind of support is appropriate.

LEADER WEEKLY CHECK-IN

Five questions before the week starts.

WHO NEEDS CLARITY?Is anyone unsure about their next step?
WHO NEEDS ENCOURAGEMENT?Who has made progress that should be recognised?
WHO NEEDS SKILL COACHING?What specific skill would unlock their next step?
WHO HAS SHOWN BUILDER BEHAVIOUR?Look for consistency, learning, action and ownership.
WHAT CAN WE SIMPLIFY?If the same question keeps appearing, improve the system or teach it once for everyone.
LEGACY LEADER

The next level isn't more control.

A Legacy Leader develops people who can eventually teach, coach and lead others. The goal isn't to build people who need you forever. It's to build people who can lead.

DO IT → LEARN IT → PRACTISE IT → TEACH IT → COACH IT → LEAD ITLeadership becomes duplicatable when the skill can move from one person to another.
